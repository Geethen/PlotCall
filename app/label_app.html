Evidence
The profile and time series are Sentinel-2 measurements for the yellow 10 m label square. Interpret the imagery first; existing land-cover maps are listed last to reduce bias.
Spectral profile
Reflectance by wavelength for the 10 m label square, with one line per year. Shortwave infrared can help separate cropped fields from permanent pasture or rough grazing.
Vegetation index through time
Annual values are shown with every usable observation behind them when all observations is on. Within-year cycles can distinguish cultivated fields from permanent pasture or rough grazing.
Existing land-cover map classifications
Reference classifications from Dynamic World, WorldCover, ESRI, GLanCE and Hansen. Check them after your own interpretation: products differ in date, resolution and class definitions, and several do not extend to 2024.
